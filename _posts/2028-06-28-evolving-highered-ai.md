AI is impacting higher education, just like it impacts the other spheres of society. How can we ride this wave?

This blob posts builds on the accumulation of [my postings in the last 4 years](https://nocodefunctions.com) on the meanings and consequences of gen AI, right from [my note on the significance of ChatGPT when it was realeased in 2022](https://nocodefunctions.com/blog/chatgpt-consequences/).

Highered has add its fair share of shocks that called for its immediate adaptation, "this or you disappear":

1. Internet and the web: the democratization and multiplication of content production by independent individuals, channels of content distribution and diversification of learning habits (shorter attention spans, mobile first, screen-based...)
2. Big data and data science: personalized learning achieved through the analysis of vast quantities of personal data, affording to create different learning paths for each student
3. MOOCs: a mix of the two first items, with massification. A few companies to flesh out the promise: Coursera, Edx, Udemy, Udacity, Khan Academy, ... these companies still exist but did not displace schools nor universities.
